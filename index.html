Baixin Xu

I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Nanyang Technological University, advised by Prof. Ying He. My research sits at the intersection of computer vision and computer graphics, with a recent focus on 3D reconstruction, neural rendering, 3D generation, and interactive world models.

Before joining NTU, I received my B.S. degree in Computer Science from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2022. During my Ph.D. at NTU, I have been working closely with Dr. Jiangbei Hu. I also spent time as a research intern at SenseTime Research and Shanghai AI Laboratory, working with Dr. Kwan-Yee Lin, and at Skywork AI, mentored by Dr. Yangguang Li.

Reconstruct geometry, generate worlds, and make them editable.
